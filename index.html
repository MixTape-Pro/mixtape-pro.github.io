MixTape Pro

Mix your music and share it online. A simple, beautiful macOS app for creating beat-matched mixes with precision and ease.

Drag and Drop Simplicity

Pull tracks from iTunes or Finder. MixTape detects tempo and beats automatically, snapping them precisely to the grid for perfect sync.

Pro Audio Controls

HiLo filter drops bass or treble for cleaner mixes. Live UI lets you hear under the playhead. Spectrogram visualizes beats and phrases with unmatched clarity.

Seamless Export and Share

Export mixes to iTunes at any bitrate for Mac, iPod, or iPhone. Publish directly to the web—no accounts or extra software needed.

Live Performance Ready

Stream live through a second audio interface while mixing in headphones. MIDI output syncs lights or VJ setups. Night mode reduces glare in dark spaces.

Effortless Workflow

Simpler than DJ apps or workstations. Blends into your Mac desktop with video tutorials and demo mixes to get you creating instantly.

How It Works

1. Import Tracks

Drag music from iTunes or Finder. Tempo, beats, and phrase markers detect automatically.

2. Build Your Mix

Drop tracks on the workspace. They snap to grid and play in perfect sync. Add envelopes for polish.

3. Refine and Export

Use HiLo, spectrogram, and live preview. Export to iTunes, share online, or go live with MIDI sync.

4. Play Anywhere

Stream to devices, web, or second interface. Night mode for low-light sessions.
